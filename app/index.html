🧱
MODULE 01

Masonry Wall Design Engine

Full parametric stone masonry wall design featuring customizable inner/outer face batter, staggered weep hole pipes, foundation soling/PCC layers, multi-unit support, and top plan/elevation views.

📦
MODULE 02

Gabion Wall Design Engine

Automated gabion box stepped wall section CAD generator. Multi-tiered cage arrangement, mesh wire parameters, boulder stone fill density calculations, backfill earth slopes, and stability checks.

🏗️
MODULE 03

RCC Retaining Wall Design Engine

RCC cantilever retaining wall (DoR standard, H 2 – 10 m): typical section, reinforcement arrangement S1–S8, bar bending schedule, dimensions, quantities (concrete, steel, formwork, filter, geotextile), DXF and print sheets.
